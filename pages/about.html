OUR PURPOSE

Turn one problem
into a solution
for the other.

Cities around the world face waste pollution and a shortage of carbon-neutral energy. Our cause is to help transform the first challenge into a solution for the second.

We recover and repurpose suitable carbon-containing waste into useful fuels and recovered materials, giving discarded resources a productive place in industry.

Our calling is to create prosperity and well-being for humanity and our planet. This ambition guides our project development in Sri Lanka and the partnerships we seek. Environmental outcomes must be established for each project through assessment and measured performance.
