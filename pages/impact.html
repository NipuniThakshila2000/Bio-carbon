What we work towards

Value beyond disposal.

The following are environmental and economic goals. They are not reported, independently verified outcomes or quantified performance claims.

01

A cleaner environment

Recovering value from suitable non-recyclable waste could help reduce reliance on landfills and open burning. That ambition depends on appropriate collection, material selection and responsible facility operation.

02

Potential carbon-footprint improvements

Recovered fuels may displace some conventional fuel use. The net climate effect depends on feedstock, process energy, emissions, transport and the displaced alternative; it cannot be inferred from recovery alone.

03

Resource recovery

Returning useful oil, gas and char to suitable applications supports a more circular approach. Recovery complements the need to prevent waste and keep recyclable materials in appropriate recycling pathways.

04

Local economic opportunities

Project development may create opportunities in collection, preparation, technical operations and industrial supply. These are potential opportunities rather than confirmed employment or investment outcomes.
