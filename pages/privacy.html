Using this website

You can browse this website without creating an account. The website does not ask for personal information unless you choose to complete an enquiry or contact BCF LANKA.

Enquiry preparation

The contact form prepares an email on your device. It does not send your details to a website database. Information entered into the form is held only in the current page and is not saved to browser storage by this website.

When you select “Prepare enquiry email,” the prepared message is passed to your email application. You decide whether to send it. Your email provider and BCF LANKA’s email systems then handle the message.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or application cookies. The hosting service may process technical information needed to deliver the site and control access.

Website hosting

The hosting service may process technical information needed to deliver the site and control access.

Website illustrations

The homepage coastline is an illustrative landscape. The equipment system image is a supplier illustration reproduced from the supplied company overview and is not evidence of an installed BCF LANKA facility.

Questions about your information

For questions about information shared with BCF LANKA, email info@bcfco.net. Do not send sensitive information to verify a request unless an appropriate method has been agreed.

This notice describes the website functionality provided here. Company-wide retention arrangements and any legal requirements should be confirmed directly with BCF LANKA.
