Our purpose
Two city challenges.
One shared purpose.
Waste pollution and the need for cleaner energy are connected challenges. BCF LANKA’s approach brings them together: recover useful fuels and materials from non-recyclable waste, and return those resources to productive use.
Our purpose is to help move Sri Lanka towards a circular economy, where materials retain value and waste becomes a resource rather than a burden.
Our approach
Recovery with a purpose.
A connected approach to materials, energy and the communities around them.
01Start with the waste challenge
Focus on non-recyclable waste and the opportunity to divert suitable materials from disposal and open burning.
02Recover useful resources
Use thermal conversion to recover oil, gas and carbon-rich material, with applications assessed against their properties.
03Work towards lasting value
Connect resource recovery with industrial needs and opportunities for local participation and economic activity.
