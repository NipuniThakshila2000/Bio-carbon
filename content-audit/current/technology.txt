Understanding pyrolysis
A different pathway
for difficult materials.
Pyrolysis is thermal decomposition in an oxygen-limited environment. Heat breaks down suitable feedstock into smaller compounds, producing gas, condensable liquids and a solid carbon-rich fraction.
BCF LANKA’s existing material describes thermal conversion of non-recyclable waste into useful fuels and materials. Feedstock assessment, operating conditions and product testing are important to understanding any specific application.
Discuss material compatibility and project-specific requirements with the technical team. This overview does not prescribe operating temperatures, capacities or product specifications.
