Oil, gas and char: three recovered fractions with potential uses beyond their first life as waste.
Discuss your requirements →
01 / Liquid resource
Pyrolysis oil
A recovered liquid fraction with potential as an alternative industrial fuel.
Characteristics
Oil is recovered by condensing part of the vapour produced during thermal conversion. Its composition depends on the feedstock and processing conditions.
Potential applications
Industrial fuel applications may be considered where the product meets equipment, quality and regulatory requirements. Suitability needs to be assessed for each use.
Ask about specifications and availability →
02 / Energy resource
Recovered gas
Recovering energy within waste for potential process use.
Characteristics
The non-condensable gas fraction is produced alongside oil and char. Its composition and usable energy depend on the materials and conversion conditions.
Potential applications
Recovered gas may provide process heat where an appropriate system is designed for it. Ask the technical team about process integration and handling requirements.
Explore process fuel opportunities →
03 / Material resource
Recovered char
A carbon-rich solid fraction with potential industrial uses.
Characteristics
The solid fraction retains carbon and other constituents from the feedstock. Composition, ash content and physical properties require characterisation.
Potential applications
Potential industrial and environmental uses depend on testing and application requirements. It should not be assumed suitable for soil use or equivalent to certified biochar.
