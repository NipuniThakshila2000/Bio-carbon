Using this website
You can browse this website without creating an account. The website does not ask for personal information unless you choose to complete an enquiry or contact BCF LANKA.
Making an enquiry
The contact form prepares an email using the name, email address, organisation, topic and message you enter. Preparation happens in your browser. The form does not submit this information to a website database or an online form-processing service.
Your email application opens with a draft, and you decide whether to send it. When you send an email, the message and associated sender information pass through your email provider and the recipient’s email service. They are then available to BCF LANKA to respond to your enquiry.
Only include information needed for the conversation. Do not include confidential business records, identity documents or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.
Cookies and browser storage
This website’s application code does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use tracking pixels, or store enquiry details in local storage. Information typed into the form remains in the current page until navigation, although your browser may independently offer autofill or retain form state.
If you use “Copy enquiry”, the prepared message is placed on your clipboard only after you choose that action.
Website hosting
The hosting provider may process standard technical request information, such as an IP address, requested URL and browser information, to deliver and secure the website. Those processes depend on the hosting configuration and provider’s policies.
Questions about your information
For questions about an enquiry you have sent, or to ask about access, correction or deletion of that information, email info@bcflanka.com
. Do not send sensitive information to verify a request unless an appropriate method has been agreed.
This notice describes the website functionality provided here. Company-wide retention arrangements and any legal requirements should be confirmed directly with BCF LANKA.
