Project focus
Lindel
Industrial Park
Sri Lanka
Lindel Industrial Park is the project location featured in BCF LANKA’s existing material. It brings the company’s resource-recovery approach into an industrial context, connecting waste preparation, thermal conversion and recovered products.
The project’s purpose is to demonstrate how suitable non-recyclable waste could become useful fuels and materials, while exploring opportunities for industrial integration.
For the current development stage, operational status, approvals, capacity or a potential site visit, please request a project update directly from BCF LANKA. The imagery on this website is illustrative and is not evidence of completion or operation.
