01 / Liquid resource

Pyrolysis oil

A recovered liquid output with potential for use in suitable industrial boilers and furnaces.

Characteristics

Composition, calorific value, contaminants, storage requirements, and end-user acceptance need assessment. Product testing establishes whether recovered oil meets the requirements of a particular industrial application. Further refining is a separate technical and commercial decision.

Potential applications

Industrial heating, subject to fuel specifications and equipment compatibility.

02 / Energy resource

Recovered gas

Recovering energy within waste for potential process use.

Characteristics

Non-condensable process gas can provide a source of reactor heating. Its composition and usable energy depend on the materials and conversion conditions.

Potential applications

Safe handling, treatment, and process controls are part of the system design.

03 / Material resource

Recovered char

A carbon-rich solid fraction with potential industrial uses.

Characteristics

The solid residue may have potential as a fuel or material input. Its composition, ash content, contamination levels and physical properties determine suitability.

Potential applications

Recovered char is not automatically equivalent to commercial carbon black. Any application depends on testing and product requirements.
